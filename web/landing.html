how a lesson goes

1

watch first

The whole dance plays once with the song, the creator's moves on screen, you in the corner.

2

learn it in parts

Each eight-count plays with the music. Brodie names the two or three moves, you dance along.

3

get your score

Your poses are matched to the creator's. One chance per part: a cheer or one concrete tip, then the next part.

under the hood

pose at 10 fps

MediaPipe body landmarks from your camera, inside Brodie's real-time coaching loop on LiveKit.

a judge, not a guess

Your last three seconds are aligned to the creator's reference with dynamic time warping; a small network trained on ten references scores the match and names the fault.
