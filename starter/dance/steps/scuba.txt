Start with both hands up by your head, palms facing out, and hold it. On four, bring your hands to your chest, then step out and sink into a wide, low stance on seven and eight.
Stay low and bouncy with your hands at your chest, then swing one arm across your body and bring a hand up to your chin.
Bring your hands up to your face, drop one hand down to your hip, then both hands back up to your face to finish.
